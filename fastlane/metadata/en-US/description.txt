You learn a language in the order you will actually need it: airport, hotel, restaurant, taxi.

Language Through Travel is a phrasebook that teaches back. Every sentence you meet along the journey enters a spaced-repetition schedule, so what you learned before the flight is still with you when you land. Interface in Vietnamese, four target languages, everything stored on your device.

FOUR LANGUAGES
English, Japanese, Korean and Thai. Each phrase comes with its Vietnamese meaning, romanisation where the script needs it, and one-tap pronunciation through your device's text-to-speech voices.

THE JOURNEY
Four stages that unlock in order — you open the hotel once the airport is half done. Each stage carries the sentences you will actually say there, single vocabulary items worth recognising, situational dialogues you can play line by line, and short culture notes for the moments a dictionary will not help with.

PHRASEBOOK
Search by the phrase itself, by its Vietnamese meaning, or by its romanisation. Filter by stage, by what you have already met, by what you have mastered. Star the ones you want at hand and they are one tap away while you are standing in the taxi queue.

PRACTICE THAT IS NOT A DRILL
• Match Up — pair phrase with meaning against the clock
• Listen Up — hear it, choose the meaning, no text to lean on
• True or False — fast two-choice judgment calls with a streak counter
• 60-Second Rush — answer as many as you can before time runs out
Every answer feeds the same review schedule, so playing a round is studying.

TRAVEL GUIDE
Six reference topics per language: emergency phrases with the local emergency numbers, counting and units, money and bargaining, directions and telling the time, the etiquette that keeps you out of trouble, and the sounds most likely to trip up a Vietnamese speaker.

PROGRESS YOU CAN SEE
A daily goal you set yourself, a streak, a thirteen-week activity map, twelve badges that are recalculated from your real progress, best scores for every game, and per-stage statistics.

BUILT TO BE QUIET
Works fully offline. No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your progress lives in your phone's own storage, one separate space per language, and never leaves the device.
